# Storyworld / franchise

## Core identity

Existing game concept: an original tower-defense game across enchanted paper scrolls, with five elemental seals, transformative upgrades, elemental reactions and folded creatures. Slogan: Fold. Combine. Conquer. This is a record of current product design, not a claim of delivered gameplay or approved lore.

**Owner's new exploratory idea:** colors/elements can differ, disagree and fight; many shared needs and relationships can also bring them together. The setting might grow beyond the first five elements through discoveries and fusions. No future direction is locked.

## Dramatic question (proposed)
Can communities that fold power differently maintain distinct identities while building a shared world? Conflict should arise from choices, interests and circumstances, not an element's inherent morality.

## Scope and creative identity
Proposed starting universe: OS-UNIV-0001. The current game remains the first possible lens into the setting. Later stories, campaigns or other genres are ideas only. Do not imply a multiverse exists simply because the framework supports one. Art direction: washi paper, ink, seal impressions and folded silhouettes. The title and slogan are existing project labels; geography, history, rulers, laws, alliances and cosmology are unapproved.

## Hard locks
None. No canon has been approved. No current gameplay redesign authorized.

## Open questions
What are seals in-world? Are the creatures natural, crafted, corrupted, or several of these? Is a color an affinity, cultural tradition, political identity, or overlapping labels? Are scrolls locations, representations, or literal worlds? Is expansion best expressed in stories or later gameplay?
