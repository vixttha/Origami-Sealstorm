# Storyworld Change Log

This log tracks **your own fictional storyworld and writing-project decisions**, not development of the Writing Universe Template itself. The source framework's engineering history remains preserved in the upstream repository's Git history and maintainer audit records. Those source release decisions are **not** your storyworld's canon.

## When you approve your first storyworld content

Record a dated, short entry under a heading such as `## YYYY-MM-DD — initial storyworld setup` with:

- The approved creator decision and why it was made.
- Every Canon ID or Source-of-Truth owner created/changed.
- Which universe/continuity/story the decision affects.
- Whether it is `WORKING`, `CANON` or `LOCKED_CANON` and where that status is recorded.
- The actual GitHub commit link or SHA if verified, plus unresolved conflicts/unknowns.

Do **not** claim a fictional fact is Canon simply because a line appears in this log. The authoritative sources are the registered owners and `CANON-REGISTRY.yaml`. ChatGPT memory and old summaries do not override them.

For a no-code, phone-friendly authoring workflow, use `docs/CHATGPT-CHAT-FIRST-WORKFLOW.md`.
