## FOLD-SEAL

A single angular folded-paper core with a small square negative-space seal, enclosed by an incomplete circular ink impression; one crease breaks the ring to suggest motion.

Directly joins origami, seals and elemental transformation. A simplified silhouette can survive at favicon size. Avoid fine brush texture in small marks.

## STORM-FOLD

Three broad paper facets pivot around a central void to form a compact rotating storm; crease geometry implies a folded paper object rather than a generic wind icon.

Expresses motion and transformation, with strong one-color performance. The seal motif may be less immediate.

## HANKO-BASTION

A folded tower rises from a small square seal frame with one folded corner and two broad defensive tiers.

Communicates tower defense instantly, but could resemble a generic castle or stamp; fine details must be avoided.

## CRANE-CREST

A soaring crane with an ink-ring trail, its folded wings forming an abstract S-shaped motion.

Strong paper-craft symbolism but may imply an air-only game and resemble familiar origami crane marks.

Recommended first concept: FOLD-SEAL. Its shape connects the name to the mechanic rather than depicting a particular element. Next: owner review and exact name/wordmark confirmation in [decision record](creative-decision.json); then artwork readiness check. No artwork generated.
