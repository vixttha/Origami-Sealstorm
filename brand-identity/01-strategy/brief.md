# Strategy and creative brief — Origami: Sealstorm

## Established project direction

An original browser tower-defense game set on enchanted paper scrolls. Players place, evolve and combine folded elemental seals against waves of origami creatures. Existing product spelling: **Origami: Sealstorm**. Existing slogan: **Fold. Combine. Conquer.** Earlier experimental title: Runic Origami & Ink. Five elements: Air (Kaze), Fire (Kagura), Water (Mizu), Earth (Tsuchi), Ink (Sumi). Forty upgrade tiers, ten reactions, six enemy archetypes, three battlefields and endless survival are planned, not released claims.

## Audience and positioning (proposed)

Strategy and tower-defense players who enjoy meaningful upgrades, combos and readable tactical systems; browser players seeking a distinctive hand-crafted aesthetic. The title screen should communicate the game genre and paper-fold fantasy within seconds. Avoid a generic mobile fantasy crest, casino aesthetic, photorealism, and unrelated Japanese stereotypes.

## First surfaces (proposed)

Title screen and repository header first, with a companion square mark for avatar and favicon. Target readable 32px icon, test 16px; target a wide title lockup at 320px and check mobile scaling. Keep the name as accessible real text wherever practical.

## Visible qualities

| Quality | Design implication |
| --- | --- |
| Crafted | Broad fold facets, controlled crease lines, restrained paper grain |
| Elemental | Five consistent gameplay accents, never all fighting for attention in the core mark |
| Tactical | Legible hierarchy, purposeful geometry, readable silhouette |
| Mythic | Abstract seal impressions and atmospheric ink, not borrowed sacred insignia |
| Dynamic | Asymmetric crease or rotational flow without visual noise |
