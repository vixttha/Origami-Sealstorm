# Logo symbol — draft direction

Lead recommendation: **FOLD-SEAL**, a broad folded-paper silhouette with an incomplete ink seal ring. Alternatives: Storm Fold, Hanko Bastion and Crane Crest. See [scored shortlist](../01-strategy/OS-CONCEPT-SHORTLIST.md). No owner lock or image exists yet.

Design requirements: one focal symbol; obvious outer silhouette; stable negative space; two or three broad crease facets; no literal five-color wheel, fake Japanese script, copied crest, excessive brush texture or tiny cutouts. Explore an ink-black master and one-color reverse first, then optional crimson seal accent. Keep the mark distinct from gameplay's five elemental icons.

Proposed review: 16/24/32/64/256px, monochrome, grayscale, light and dark, transparent edges, centered square crop, legible at avatar size. Initial clear space: one-quarter symbol width on all sides, subject to optical review. Deliver mark only, combined lockups and one-color variants as registered PNGs after gate approval. Optional vector master is a separately reviewed future workflow, not a substitute for required PNGs.
