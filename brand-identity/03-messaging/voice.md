# Slogan and voice — Origami: Sealstorm

**Existing slogan:** Fold. Combine. Conquer.

**Proposed one-line descriptor:** A tower-defense game where folded elemental seals evolve and combine across enchanted paper scrolls.

**Tone:** concise, tactical, imaginative and crafted. Use plain verbs for controls, concrete effect descriptions and measured claims. Avoid invented accolades, promises of perfection, unsupported release dates and excessive lore in combat tooltips.

| Surface | Proposed copy |
| --- | --- |
| README opener | Place elemental seals, unfold new attack forms and discover powerful reactions in an origami-inspired tower-defense game. |
| Title screen | Fold your defenses. Combine your elements. |
| Build hint | Choose a seal, then place it on an open area of the scroll. |
| Invalid placement | Cannot place a seal here. Choose a clear area away from the enemy path. |
| Discovery | New reaction discovered. Open the Scroll of Seals to learn its effect. |
| Experimental status | Independent prototypes are being evaluated; features remain subject to testing and approval. |

Use slogan on title, README hero and promotional cards; not every button or tooltip. Keep [naming](../02-naming/naming.md) consistent. The [product README](https://github.com/vixttha/Origami-Sealstorm/blob/bce4095da1dd9cb9ff9d34afb61f157fe09a5ae7/README.md) is evidence of current planned scope, not finished gameplay.
