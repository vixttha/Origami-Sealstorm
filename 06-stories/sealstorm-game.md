# Story bible — initial tower-defense game

## Existing medium and premise
Browser tower defense: defend sanctuaries across enchanted paper scrolls, place and evolve towers, discover elemental reactions, fight waves of folded creatures. Current candidate archetypes are Paper Moth, Folded Crane, Origami Fox, Tidal Serpent, Iron-Fold Tortoise and Paper Dragon. Their narrative status (species, constructs, enemies by circumstance, or other) is unknown.

## Proposed narrative engine
A threatened sanctuary might need rival elemental traditions to work together; defending a shared place can reveal new combinations and challenge prejudices. Alternatives: the campaign could be a training trial, defense of trade routes, or a conflict over corrupted seals. No chosen antagonist, inciting event, protagonist, ending or historical cause. The player might be a seal artisan, commander or another role; undecided.

## Boundaries
Gameplay remains first; story may initially be environmental details and codex entries. Future campaigns, a broader adventure, a comic or other media are exploratory, not promised. Do not assign factions to enemies by default. Do not infer lore canon from a wave table, an endless run or a prototype ending. No official story arc or release commitment.
