# Visual production specification — provisional

Existing brand direction: origami folds, washi paper, ink strokes, seal impressions and five distinct elemental color cues. Prototype colors: Air teal, Fire red, Water blue, Earth tan, Ink near-black. Proposed brand palette is not locked. Colors may represent affinity without prescribing personality or morality; mixed communities may use several colors. Fusions could combine shape and material cues rather than just blending hues. Make silhouettes and labels distinct without reliance on color alone.

No immutable visual anchors, approved faction symbols, character designs, map art or logo. Cultural accuracy and actual production assets require review. Brand authority stays in [Brand-Identity](https://github.com/vixttha/Origami-Sealstorm/tree/Brand-Identity), not this story record.
