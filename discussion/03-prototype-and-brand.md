# Existing prototype and brand reference

## Product identity (existing repository text, not a new lore lock)

Origami: Sealstorm — Fold. Combine. Conquer. An original tower-defense concept combining Japanese paper-craft artistry, transformative elemental towers and strategic combat across enchanted paper scrolls, defending sanctuaries from waves of folded creatures. Earlier experimental name Runic Origami & Ink. Product is in experimental foundation stage. Four prototypes evaluated independently; original test prompt was created quickly, is not binding on the final public product.

| Element | Label | Combat emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air | Kaze | Homing, piercing, displacement |
| Fire | Kagura | Explosion, burning, area damage |
| Water | Mizu | Ricochet, whirlpools, freezing |
| Earth | Tsuchi | Heavy impact, armor breaking, shattering |
| Ink | Sumi | Crowd control, staining, linking |

Five families, two paths per family, forty transformative tiers, ten advanced evolutions, ten pair reactions, six layered enemies, three battlefields, three difficulties, Infinite Scroll, Corrupted Ink modifiers, procedural art/audio and persistent player statistics. Candidate difficulties 25/35/40 waves; six archetypes Paper Moth, Folded Crane, Origami Fox, Tidal Serpent, Iron-Fold Tortoise, Paper Dragon. Enemy layers peel/split. Prototype armor/camo counterplay, crosspaths, ink economy/salvage, placement and targeting, codex discovery. Map names: Winding Calligraphy River, Twin-Bridge Crossover, The Spiral Shrine. Stack proposed TypeScript, React, Vite, HTML5 Canvas, Tailwind, Web Audio, Playwright and GitHub Actions; Neon is standalone HTML. No official foundation selected in this discussion. Long-session return/save-resume is an adjacent owner requirement from prototype work, not proven by local statistics.

## Brand direction draft

Current name and slogan above. Nine populated sections: strategy, naming, messaging, logo, wordmark, visual system, applications, review, assets. Audience proposed strategy/TD players and browser users; title screen and README hero first; 32px icon with 16px test, title lockup 320px with 160px narrow test. Crafted, tactical, mythic, dynamic. Paper texture, sumi-e brush, origami geometry, seal impressions. Avoid generic fantasy crest, casino aesthetic, photorealism, fake script, copied sacred insignia, illegible creases, red/green-only signals. Keep title readable as text. Japanese labels/language and rights need review.

| Concept | Fit 35% | Art 25% | Distinctiveness 25% | Versatility 15% | Total |

Fold Seal is agent provisional lead, not locked. Emblem lead; abstract/pictorial alternatives; wordmark complementary; mascot risks narrowing identity. Proposed exact wordmark Origami: Sealstorm, paired confirmation null. Palette proposals: Washi #F5EBDD, Sumi #1D1E2C, Seal Crimson #9E2939, Muted Gold #B88A45; prototype element cues Air #2A9D8F, Fire #E63946, Water #3A86FF, Earth #D4A373, Ink #1D1E2C. Sumi/washi calculated contrast 13.98:1; full UI accessibility not verified. Proposed title display lettering with subtle fold cuts, neutral readable UI sans, tabular numbers. No font chosen or licensed. Element silhouettes wing/fan, flame/lantern, koi/wave, shard, ink droplet/brush.

Asset plan (0/13 categories produced): master logo; master wordmark; horizontal lockup; stacked lockup; monochrome dark; monochrome light; avatar; README hero; title screen; favicon; five elemental icons; social preview; optional animation. Suggested exports title 1600px, README 1600×600, avatar 512×512, favicon 64px checked at 16px, social 1200×630. Check exact text, silhouette, spacing, clear space, light/dark, transparency, one color, accessibility, visual similarity, rights, reduced motion. No artwork generated/approved, no trademark screening or rollout. Inspiration images were illustrative references only, not part of product assets.

Brand voice: concise, tactical, imaginative, crafted. Proposed descriptor: A tower-defense game where folded elemental seals evolve and combine across enchanted paper scrolls. Example build hint: Choose a seal, then place it on an open area of the scroll. Invalid placement: Cannot place a seal here. Choose a clear area away from the enemy path. Discovery: New reaction discovered. Open the Scroll of Seals to learn its effect. Existing README claims are plans not finished capability.
