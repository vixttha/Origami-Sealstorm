# Unified seal architecture — latest discussion

All details are exploratory unless explicitly identified in [approval ledger](09-approval-ledger.md).

## Three owner-originated systems

1. Bound Origami: example 100 different physical figures with individual base stats and characteristics. Different elemental seals awaken/evolve the same figure differently. A bound figure might become a living companion, be unbound and die in battle. Questions: compatibility, rarity, collection, attachment, permanent development, death/recovery, replacement. Figure count illustrative.
2. Manifestation Talismans: write a seal on a talisman, throw it into battle, manifest a tower, ability, beast or other form determined by seal and element. Owner proposed single use. Different from persistent bound companions; no assumption all seals consumable.
3. Wielder Mastery: fight to level up and pursue seal skill paths. Possibly all start with five elements and specialize in two, three, all five or other combinations by playstyle. Later intrinsic two-element origami occupying one spot, two neighboring manifestations combining, three adjacent manifestations creating huge tower or other greater form. No locked skill tree, caps or exact positional rule.

These may coexist in world laws without all becoming first-game systems. The product need not follow the original 10-minute test prompt.

## Synthesis

Ancient elemental language → seal makers refine inscriptions + Ink Masters practice Ink Control → physical elemental seals → origami figure and wielder → elemental manifestation, depending on available energy.

Working definition V2 (not locked): A seal is a physical inscription written in an ancient elemental language, carrying knowledge accumulated and refined across generations of seal makers and wielders. When activated, its inscription channels elemental energy into an origami figure or prepared talisman. The resulting manifestation is influenced by the inscription's characteristics, the physical form receiving it, the wielder's proficiency and the energy available. Seals can awaken stronger abilities through battle, interact with compatible seals and potentially create new manifestations through elemental fusion.

Reusable knowledge is not automatically a reusable physical object. Talisman consumption may differ from seal removal. Distinguish inscription identity, physical medium, form, animation and power.

## Five factors of power (assistant architecture)

| Factor | Possible contribution |
| --- | --- |
| Origami figure (O) | Physical base stats, shape, natural abilities, evolution potential |
| Seal (S) | Inscription quality, complexity, element, inherited knowledge |
| Wielder (W) | Mastery, concentration, specialization, technique |
| Battle energy (E) | Temporary stored/allocated energy, upgrades |
| Synergy (R) | Resonance, reactions, support, fusion |

Conceptually P=f(O,S,W,E,R). Not a numerical formula, no arbitrary multipliers. Damage, speed, durability, range and abilities may need separate calculations. Owner explicitly wants seal and figure strength contributions, not just in-battle upgrades.

Illustrative same base wolf: Fire Wolf burns/explodes; Water Wolf fluid movement/slows; Earth Wolf heavy/durable; Ink Wolf stains/links; Air variant not specified. Examples not approved designs. 100 figures × 5 elements = 500 potential single variants, 100 × 10 pairs = 1,000 potential two-element variants if universally compatible. General n figures, e elements: n×e and n×e(e−1)/2. Actual art/animation/balance burden large; compatibility can deliberately restrict scope.

## Ink

Owner idea: ink may be precious world currency, also used for writing seals and battle upgrades. Handling it is difficult; trained professionals called Ink Masters practice Ink Control. Do not confuse them with Ink Wielders (combat elemental affinity). Fire Wielder could become Ink Master; Ink Wielder might not be trained in professional ink handling. Profession and affinity are distinct. Preserve this owner distinction but full laws/economy not decided. Ink could serve four roles: currency, crafting material, battle resource, cultural/knowledge preservation. Need decide why scarce, how acquired, prepared, controlled, traded, conserved, spent, counterfeit-resistant, whether all ink is same substance. Physical ink vs energy not decided.

Ten proposed power names: Sealforce (channeled power); Resonance (compatible interaction energy); Essence (fundamental power); Foldforce (animation/transformation); Imprint (retained inscription power); Vital Ink (energized ink); Elemental Flux (flow); Inscription Charge (temporary store); Spirit Current (flow through manifestations); Aether (underlying supernatural power). Assistant preference Foldforce or Essence if distinct from Ink. None locked.

## Combined progression model

Energy absorption (defeated enemies/other battlefield events release recoverable energy) → elemental saturation (accumulate in active seal/figure) → wielder concentration (direct/allocate energy) → battle awakening (advanced forms, inscriptions and abilities). The owner likes combining all three; the exact implementation remains open.

Possible persistence: figure base stats, seal properties, learned inscriptions and wielder mastery permanent; companion experience potentially permanent; battle upgrades, saturation and battlefield resonance temporary; combined manifestations depend on fusion type. Need explain loss, death, postbattle resets, what survives unbinding, why ink currency upgrades do not imply permanent loss of knowledge or permanent accumulation of combat energy. Preserve existing game economy as evidence, not binding decision.

## Three provisional fusion classes

| Class | Footprint | Concept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Intrinsic fusion | One position | Two-element fused seal/figure with independent identity/attack |
| Linked fusion | Two positions | Nearby manifestations connect for coordinated power or larger combined form |
| Greater fusion | Three or more positions | Formation, combined attack or enormous tower/guardian |

Examples Air+Earth Sand intrinsic form, Fire+Water linked, Fire+Earth+Air triangle greater formation. Not approved recipes, thresholds or geometry. Distinguish from target reactions and proximity indicators. New independent elements can enter without derivation, and fusions may reproduce existing powers in different ways.

Foundation: prototype TD, seals, elements, reactions, temporary upgrades.
Expanded strategy: independent figures and seal properties, resonance, fusion.
Persistent progression: companions, collection, mastery, skill trees, inherited techniques.
Expanded universe: professions, NPCs, crafting, trade, civilizations, exploration, stories and potential other game genres.

Owner wants public product that makes sense within its own world laws. The world can be expansive without requiring all mechanics in the first game. No implementation commitments made.
