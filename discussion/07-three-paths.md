# Three paths — prior synthesis

## Core interpretation

The seal and origami figure are distinct. The seal empowers a physical origami creation. Preferred foundation: physical talisman (option 1), ancient language (7), inherited knowledge (8). Optional mechanics: resonance, fusion, support, battle progression, summons, retrieval. Optional future expansions: more elements, collectible creatures, living plants, golems, character pedestals, spirits, techniques and campaigns. The owner approved the prior categorization for discussion, not all mechanics.

## Shared questions

Acquisition: distinguish seal knowledge, physical seal, receiving origami figure, battle resource. In-world professions and production do not necessarily require an in-game crafting simulator. Learning/unlocking an inscription can differ from manufacturing a consumable object. Buying, selling, making, trade and collection optional depending on world laws/complexity.

Temporary growth alternatives:
- Energy absorption: enemies release usable energy, stored power dissipates.
- Seal activation: unlock advanced inscriptions, return to dormancy.
- Origami transformation: resource investment enables complex folds, figure unfolds.
- Elemental saturation: energy accumulates, excess discharges.
- Wielder concentration: direct power to seals, wielder recovers.

Owner favors absorption + saturation + concentration. Permanent knowledge and mastery can remain while in-battle power resets. Existing ink upgrades should not silently become kill-based XP; kills may instead explain available resources.

Access, affinity, mastery, specialization distinct: ability to use an element, natural understanding, proficiency and chosen focus. A multi-element wielder may specialize in one; single-element specialists may cooperate. No decision on universal access.

Retrieval alternatives: permanent placement; free pickup/redeploy; limited pickup with cost/cooldown; between-wave relocation; salvage/destruction with partial refund. Decide what is retrieved (seal, figure, energy, all), and whether accumulated power survives. Original prototypes have salvage, not proof of unrestricted pickup.

## Six manifestation categories

Stationary Constructs, Living Constructs, Summoned Creatures, Elemental Guardians, Support Constructs, Fused Constructs. Owner later explicitly locked category structure. Summoning may be an ability, specialized seal family, or future element; not decided. Examples are plants, golems, periodic animal summons, water serpents grabbing enemies, allies strengthened by support, combined forms.

## Path A — Art of Inscription

Physical seal written in ancient magical language, determines elemental power channeled into origami. Generations discover symbols and profound inscriptions. Knowledge made physical. Learn/inherit/discover; no mandatory individual crafting in combat; five elements possible through five known inscriptions; upgrade activates advanced layers; temporary energy powers stronger manifestations and dissipates/unfolds after combat; compatible writing creates fusions; retrieval potentially loses active power; new elements via discovery of symbols. Fire figure → paper dragon as additional ink activates layers. Strength: close to origami premise, flexible and simple. Risk: language needs limits or becomes explanation for anything.

## Path B — Awakening of Seals

Dormant reservoir/channel of elemental energy, inscription determines identity, awakening power determines active strength. Inherit/receive/discover dormant reusable seals; multiple compatible seals may be activated; upgrade through accumulated energy; energy dissipates after combat; resonance transfers/combines energy; retrieval potentially loses charge; new energies can be discovered. Fire + Earth charged neighbors might produce Molten Slag or another combined form. Strength: progression, reset, proximity logic. Risk: extra energy economy complexity; cannot make battle upgrades only power factor.

## Path C — Keepers of the Seals

Accumulated generational knowledge of wielders, craftsmen, masters. Specialists teach, share creations and combine expertise. Player may access contributions without personally mastering all elements. Relationships/inheritance/discovery; specialists may make seals offscreen; upgraded techniques become available during battle, advanced forms require temporary resources; shared methods make fusions; valuable inherited seals potentially retrievable; new element access through trust, alliance or lost traditions. Initial five allied defenders, later Lightning group shares techniques after meaningful reconciliation. Optional NPC pedestal buffs. Strength: story and history. Risk: compulsory character management or excessive NPC statistics.
