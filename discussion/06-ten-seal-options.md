# Ten possible definitions of a seal

All options were brainstorming, none selected as sole definition.

| # | Option | Meaning / potential | Owner's response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Elemental Talisman | Enchanted paper inscription activates a defense; could be crafted, traded, collected | Likes physical seal, wants option 7 as background; acquisition/crafting burden and origami separation concern |
| 2 | Spirit Vessel | Contains elemental spirit with personality; temporary origami body, growth through bonds | Interesting, but weapons/vessels, stats, effects, acquisition, multi-element access and consumability add complexity; risks losing origami identity |
| 3 | Arcane Blueprint | Magical instructions shape elemental energy; advanced inscription creates complex dragon, fusion combines instructions | Makes sense; questions additional stats and existing levels |
| 4 | Elemental Covenant | Agreement with elemental power, responsibilities and access | Concern people may only hold one or two, undermining access to all five/combinations |
| 5 | Elemental Seed | Concentrated power grows with resources; nearby seeds interact | Good for specific giant plant manifestations and proximity, not whole origami system |
| 6 | Elemental Gateway | Connection to external energy source; stronger channel | Better as low-damage support seal empowering two or more connected seals; concern about one-element restriction |
| 7 | Language of Creation | Ancient words/symbols command reality; combinations produce new instructions | Favored as lore foundation with option 1 |
| 8 | Elemental Legacy | Knowledge, techniques and experience passed across generations | Likes generational writing, every maker/wielder adds depth/profoundness |
| 9 | Elemental Instrument | Engineered tool captures/directs energy; networks and invention | Maybe optional NPC on battlefield pedestal adding elemental/stat bonus; iffy, another variable |
| 10 | Living Seal | Living organism that folds/unfolds, develops personality, hybrid evolution | Better as possible summon ability or future element; periodic animals, plants, golems, elementals, collection, hundreds of animals, water serpents grab and wiggle enemies |
