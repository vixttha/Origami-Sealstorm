# Expansion of elemental interactions

## Existing game reactions
Prototype design records ten pair reactions; they are not yet proof of a permanent fused element.

| Pair | Existing reaction concept |
| --- | --- |
| Water + Fire | Scalding Steam |
| Water + Air | Monsoon Squall |
| Water + Earth | Mudslide |
| Water + Ink | Spreading Wash |
| Fire + Air | Firestorm |
| Fire + Earth | Molten Slag |
| Fire + Ink | Scorched Lacquer |
| Air + Earth | Sandstorm Shrapnel |
| Air + Ink | Calligraphy Gale |
| Earth + Ink | Petrified Seal |

## Future possibilities (not locked)
- Reaction: temporary interaction between distinct elements, useful for teamwork or combat.
- Fusion technique: a deliberately combined form with a distinct purpose, cost and limitation.
- Hybrid material / creature: a lasting result of a combination, if the setting supports it.
- Derived affinity: a rare stable discipline emerging from combined practices; not automatically a base element.
- New base affinity: a genuinely distinct force that cannot be reduced to an existing reaction; the roster is not capped.

Examples for exploration only: steam as a cooperative heating or propulsion craft; mixed ink and water as a communication or restoration medium; earth and fire as fired-paper armor or a forged defensive form. Possible additional independent affinities to investigate include lightning, frost, plant, metal or light, without adopting any or fixing their count. Some may instead be techniques, materials or existing derivatives. Explore multi-element combinations only if meaningful; avoid combinatorial bloat and duplicates.

## Evaluation questions
What does each parent contribute? Is there a distinctive use outside combat? What creates the fusion, sustains it and breaks it? Can rival traditions contribute willingly? Is the result understandable to players? What limitations, reversibility, rarity and counterplay prevent it dominating existing elements? What lore consequences follow from discovery?

## Hard locks
None. No expansion, fusion recipe, element list, mechanics or release commitment approved. The original five-element tower-defense milestone is unchanged.
