# Elemental seal system

## Existing design reference (not approved lore)
Air / Kaze, Fire / Kagura, Water / Mizu, Earth / Tsuchi, Ink / Sumi. Five tower families, two upgrade paths per family, ten pair reactions. Prototype statuses: Wind-Touched, Burning, Soaked, Fractured, Stained. The current game uses ink as a resource; its exact fictional meaning is unknown.

## Proposed operating principle
Folded forms and written seals might shape elemental energy through material, motion and skill. Alternatives: seals could awaken existing forces, encode agreements with entities, or simply be crafted tools. Choose no origin yet. Potential costs: paper durability, ink use, training, environmental constraints and recovery; not balance numbers or immutable laws.

## Openness
Five is the current game roster, not necessarily the limit of nature. Affinity is not a moral alignment or necessarily a social faction. People may have multiple affinities or none; undecided. No new base element is selected. Define limits only after comparing narrative and gameplay needs.

## Hard locks
None. Existing gameplay balance and prototype evidence remain separate. See OS-SYS-0002 for the exploratory distinction between reactions and fusions.
