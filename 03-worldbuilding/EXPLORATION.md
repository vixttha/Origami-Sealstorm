# Elemental societies — exploratory relationship map

All entries below are options, not canon. Treat colors as overlapping traditions, not five mandatory homogeneous countries. Individuals and organizations can cooperate, disagree internally, move across borders and practice multiple traditions. Rivalry and friendship can coexist.

| Pair | Possible tension | Possible common ground |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Air–Fire | Spreading fires, dangerous winds | Controlled heat and airflow, rescue |
| Air–Water | Storms and navigation disputes | Trade routes, rain management |
| Air–Earth | Free movement versus borders | Bridge engineering, safe travel |
| Air–Ink | Oral freedom versus controlled records | Messaging and shared maps |
| Fire–Water | Competition over heat and water | Steam craft, disaster recovery |
| Fire–Earth | Extraction and destructive heat | Kilns, rebuilding, durable structures |
| Fire–Ink | Burning records, disputed seal use | Controlled printing and ceremony |
| Water–Earth | River diversion, erosion | Irrigation, flood control, bridges |
| Water–Ink | Loss or dilution of records | Wash craft, restoration, communication |
| Earth–Ink | Monument ownership, disputed boundaries | Archives, seal inscriptions, shared infrastructure |

Possible unifying forces: shared sanctuaries, trade, tournaments, disasters, common threats, cross-tradition families, apprenticeships, collective discoveries and rebuilding after conflicts. None of these need be a permanent enemy or universal peace treaty. An emerging hybrid technique might be invented by two rival groups, creating a dilemma about shared knowledge. The first five elements are a starting point, not a cosmological cap.

Open design choices: are colors social, magical, both or neither? What controls access to affinity? What is a fusion versus a reaction or material? How are unaligned people represented? What scale (a village, region, world, or multiple scroll realms) supports the actual game? What conflicts are local versus setting-wide? What makes cooperation credible rather than forced?

No new elements, leaders, factions, names, cosmology or stories locked. Follow [system record](systems/elemental-seals.md) and [fusion proposal](systems/reactions-and-fusions.md).
