# LLM Worker Rules

- Resolve authority through `CANON-REGISTRY.yaml`.
- Stay inside assigned scope.
- Use existing Canon IDs rather than inventing duplicate owners.
- Mark new unapproved material `PROPOSAL` or `WORKING`.
- Preserve unknowns as unknowns.
- Report contradictions instead of choosing a preferred version.
- Do not promote your own major work without separate review.
- Do not treat confident prose, old prompts, chat exports, or generated summaries as authority unless registered.
- Do not collapse alternate universes/timelines into one record.

**Absence of information is not permission to invent approved canon.**
