# Anti-Slop Standard

Reject or revise material that introduces generic genre language in place of owned mechanics; fake scientific precision; arbitrary dramatic numbers; convenient one-scene powers/rules/history; duplicate authorities; same-voice characters; unnatural exposition; random crossover references; consequence-free escalation; disappearing injury/trauma; unsupported superlatives; or lore created merely to fill space.

Every meaningful addition must answer:

**Why does this exist? What owns it? What scope does it apply to? What does it affect? What does it cost?**

If the answer is only “it sounds cool,” keep it as a proposal.
