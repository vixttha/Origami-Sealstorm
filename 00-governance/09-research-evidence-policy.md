# Research & Evidence Policy

Real-world research and fictional canon are separate evidence classes.

- **R1 — Primary/authoritative external source**
- **R2 — Strong secondary source**
- **R3 — Internal storyworld canon/Decision Record**

Prefer R1 for externally verifiable claims. Record source, access date, supported claim, and where it is used. State uncertainty rather than converting inference into fact.

External research may inform canon but becomes fictional canon only through deliberate approval. Fictional canon cannot prove a real-world claim.
