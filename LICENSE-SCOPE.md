You may create a **private or public Creator Project repository** from the official template. You may keep the Framework's Markdown/YAML/schema files in it, modify them for your fiction, share the genuine creative project with collaborators, and permit normal viewing, cloning, and forking. This applies even when the original creative work has a commercial purpose. Downstream project collaborators and legitimate project forks receive the same **limited Framework permissions directly under `LICENSE`**.

When sharing a repository, keep the Framework's license/copyright notices and distinguish reusable Framework files from your original creative work. This permission does **not** mean that other users are allowed to copy or commercialize your original fiction without your own permission. It does not give users permission to claim ownership of the Framework.
